These terms govern the use of the Control Electoral system. Using the platform means accepting them: acceptance is recorded with the version, the timestamp, the IP address, and the browser it was made from.

1. Purpose and description of the service

Control Electoral is an observation and results-recording platform for the Province of Santa Elena, Ecuador. It lets registered observers document polling station tally sheets, attach photographic evidence, and lets coordinators resolve the incidents raised against those tally sheets.

The service is provided as is, without a guarantee of uninterrupted availability: there may be maintenance windows or technical incidents.

2. Acceptable use

By using the platform, a person undertakes to:

  • Record only tally sheets and evidence they have actually observed, without altering, completing, or inventing data.
  • Capture evidence at the place and time of the observation, without photographing people, identity documents, or any third-party data that is not needed to evidence the tally sheet.
  • Use their credentials only for themselves, and not share the password or access code with anyone else.
  • Not attempt to access tally sheets, incidents, or personal data outside the scope of their permissions.
  • Immediately report any unauthorised use of their credentials, or any anomaly that compromises the traceability of the data.
  • Comply with applicable electoral law and the coordinator's instructions.

3. User responsibilities

  • Accuracy: they are responsible for what they record. A tally sheet with wrong data compromises the result and may be invalidated.
  • Confidentiality: screenshots and exports must not be shared with unauthorised people, even when the person has legitimate access to the data.
  • Credential protection: the password and access code are personal and non-transferable.
  • Incident reporting: any unauthorised account use or data-integrity failure must be reported immediately.

4. Roles and permissions

Access depends on the assigned role: observer, coordinator, administrator, and master. Each role sees and can modify a different set of data. The system enforces access control on the server and in the database; an attempt outside the permitted scope is rejected and logged.

The personal data the system processes, with its legal basis and retention periods, is set out in the Privacy Policy.

5. Intellectual property

The software, the electoral database, and the system documentation belong to their respective owners and are used under licence. The tally sheets, evidence, and incidents recorded by observers are electoral process data, and their use is governed by applicable law.

6. Limitation of liability

The system is provided "as is". To the extent permitted by applicable law, there is no liability for data loss caused by failures outside the system (network, device, or third parties), nor for decisions taken on the basis of information incorrectly recorded by the observer themselves. Responsibility for the record lies with whoever made it.

7. Suspension and account closure

  • Closure: account deletion can be requested through the DPO contact form.
  • Data that is not deleted: tally sheets, evidence, and audit trails are kept for the period required by electoral law (around 7 years). They are part of the process record and do not depend on the account.
  • Suspension: an account may be suspended for breach of these terms, for attempted unauthorised access, or at the request of the electoral authority.

These terms are versioned. When a new version is published, the system stops treating the previous one as accepted, and **every person must accept again before continuing to use the platform**. The date and version number appear at the top of this document, and the acceptance history is kept on record.
