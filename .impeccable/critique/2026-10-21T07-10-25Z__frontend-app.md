## Design Specificity Verdict

The domain thinking is genuinely authored: the checksum is a first-class concept across four surfaces, the six-state acta lifecycle is real, the forensic chain (evidence + CNE PDF + transition history with actor, IP and integrity checklist) is there, and the consequence copy is honest. That part is not a template.

The visual system, though, is a stock Sakai/Aura template with an electoral skin. Nothing declares a `font-family`, so the app's own strings render in the OS default serif while the PrimeNG chrome renders in Inter — two type families on all fourteen routes, visible in every screenshot. The institutional navy sidebar documented in DESIGN.md was dropped in favour of the theme surface, the shipped type scale (36/24/15/12) is not the documented one (56/24/14/12), the radii (6/8/12/16) are not the documented ones (6/10/14/18), and the status vocabulary grew a purple that DESIGN.md does not know about.

The migration left three token vocabularies alive at once: PrimeNG's `--p-*` (80 references, 9 files), the app's `--color-*` (157 references, 15 files) and an abandoned legacy set (19 references, 0 definitions). That third vocabulary is why thirteen undefined custom properties survived the migration unnoticed.

### Deterministic scan

The bundled detector over the whole `frontend/app/src` tree returned 14 findings across four rules: `side-tab` x8 (all real — coloured borders above 1px on rounded cards and table rows, an explicit craft-floor ban), `layout-transition` x2 (real — `margin-left` and `width` are animated, so every sidebar toggle reflows), `border-accent-on-rounded` x1 (real, `dashboard.page.scss:95`), and `broken-image` x3 (false positives — Angular's `[src]` binding is invisible to the static HTML engine).

The browser overlay produced 53 low-contrast, 35 layout-transition, 15 ai-color-palette, 6 tight-leading, 4 cramped-padding, 2 tiny-text and 2 skipped-heading findings. All 29 `text-occlusion` results were the detector detecting its own overlay labels and were discarded after purging the overlay elements.

## Overall Impression

The migration produced a competent, consistent shell around a feature layer that was never finished. The PrimeNG work is good: the components are used correctly, the tables and dialogs are idiomatic, the token preset is well documented and the pagination, the badges and the pagination behaviour are sound. What is missing is the layer between the library and the product: a dark scheme, a typeface, and a coherent token contract. On top of that sit three defects that are not design problems at all and are worse: a crash I introduced in the last PR, a role check that reads a storage key nobody writes, and an offline queue that deletes the observer's acta.

The single biggest opportunity is the token layer. Every one of the eight invisible text pairs, the 25 contrast failures, the missing font and the 13 undefined properties have the same root cause and the same fix.

## What's Working

- **The domain vocabulary is precise and consistent where it matters.** `BORRADOR`, `ENVIADA`, `EN_DISPUTA`, `CORREGIDA`, `APROBADA` are a real lifecycle, the badge carries text plus icon plus colour, and the "auditar, balance y evidencia" framing tells a coordinator exactly what the screen is for.
- **The PrimeNG adoption is clean.** `p-table` with the paginator, `p-dialog` for the confirmations, `p-tag` for the states, `p-toast` for the outcomes — the library is used the way it is meant to be, and the `app-*` primitives in `shared/ui/` are a coherent seven-component vocabulary.
- **The light status palette is already correct.** Measured 7.29:1 to 8.01:1 for every state pair, with tinted surfaces and matching borders. It does not need touching; the dark counterpart has to be authored to the same discipline.

## Priority Issues

- **[P0] The dark scheme does not exist.** `src/theme/tokens.scss` declares a single light-only `:root`; `grep -r app-dark` returns 0 hits in the SCSS. Flipping `.app-dark` moves only PrimeNG's half of the surface, so the app's own text stays `#0f172a` on a `#0f172a` card: eight pairs measure **1.00:1** and the glyphs are not painted at all (`h3.app-card__title`, `span.info-value`, `label.form-label`, `span.candidate-name`), and 25 pairs fail in total. `src/theme/typography.scss:23-48` compounds it by forcing `h1..h6` and `p` colours, which also breaks the blue banner in the *light* scheme (`dashboard.page.scss:7-16` measures 2.93:1 for the heading and 2.12:1 for the caption). Fix: author the `.app-dark` block with the same ramp PrimeNG's dark surfaces use, lightened foregrounds and translucent status surfaces, and scope the typography rule so coloured containers keep their own text colour. → `/impeccable colorize`, then `/impeccable typeset`

- **[P0] The acta detail crashed on every visit (fixed in this run).** The transition-history card and the evidence zoom dialog read the loaded detail outside the guard that checks it, so the first render, with the detail still null, threw `TypeError: Cannot read properties of null`. Fixed in the same branch as this review, with a rendering spec that loads the detail asynchronously so an unguarded read fails the build. → `/impeccable harden`

- **[P0] The observer's vote input is clipped and the checksum is below the fold.** `.candidate-input{width:90px}` does not constrain the inner number input, so it overflows its own wrapper and the card — at 1440px and at 390px. The banner that gates "Continuar" sits one to two screens below the input it validates. For a product whose product principle #3 is "resaltar visualmente alertas matemáticas", the alert is both invisible and unreachable. → `/impeccable adapt`, then `/impeccable layout`

- **[P1] The MASTER's powers are invisible.** `detectarRolMaster()` reads `sessionStorage.getItem('app.user.roles')`, and nothing in `src/` ever writes that key — the grep for `setItem` returns only the theme, the drafts, the PIN and the sidebar. `esMaster` is therefore permanently false, so "Anular acta", "Crear usuario", enable/disable, "anular usuario" and "Revocar sesión" never render, while the topbar says MASTER. The JWT already carries the role and `roleGuard` uses it. → `/impeccable harden`

- **[P1] The offline queue can destroy an election record and can duplicate one.** `sync.service.ts:141-148` increments the retry counter for *any* error and, at five, calls `eliminarPendiente(item.id)` — the acta and its photo blob are deleted from IndexedDB with no user-visible notice, and the docstring claims only a 4xx is abandoned. Separately, `:110-140` can POST the acta successfully, fail on the image, and re-POST the acta on the next retry with no idempotency key. Five 30-second connectivity gaps destroy the record; one flaky image upload duplicates it. → `/impeccable harden`

- **[P1] The mobile topbar paints over itself.** At 390px the actions block is ~394px wide inside a 366px content box with `flex-shrink:0`, and the wordmark cannot truncate, so "En línea · Conectado" lands on top of "Control Electoral" — the wordmark reads "ctrol" — and the notification badge covers the bell. Visible in `m-dashboard-light.png`, `m-coordinator-actas-light.png` and `m-observer-registrar-light.png`. → `/impeccable adapt`

- **[P1] The product has no typeface.** Nothing declares `font-family`, so the app's own strings render in the UA serif while PrimeNG's chrome renders in Inter, on every route. `typography.scss:14-21` applies `tabular-nums` to five decorative selectors and to **none** of the vote counts, percentages or ballot figures, which PRODUCT.md calls sacred. → `/impeccable typeset`

- **[P1] Six of fifteen routes are placeholders that do not say so.** `/evidencias`, `/evidencias/subir`, `/master/cargos`, `/master/partidos`, `/master/candidatos` and `/actas` all render the same stub, and the route data overrides the stub's honest "Módulo en construcción" with a description of a feature that does not exist. The sidebar advertises four of them as working. → `/impeccable onboard`, then `/impeccable shape`

- **[P1] The locale is en-US, so one acta shows two dates.** No `LOCALE_ID` and no `registerLocaleData('es')` anywhere: the mobile card renders `10/21/26, 12:45 AM` while the desktop table renders `21/10/2026 00:45` for the same record. Number separators would be `1,234.56` rather than `1.234,56`. → `/impeccable clarify`

- **[P2] Thirteen custom properties are referenced and never defined, eleven of them live.** `--font-size-2xl` makes the page `<h1>` render *smaller* than the `<h3>` card titles under it; `--primitive-slate-500` makes the status border render `0px solid`; the success tokens strip the checksum banner of both its background and its border, so the mathematical alert has no container. → `/impeccable colorize`

- **[P2] The observer can dead-end with no explanation.** With zero votes, `isChecksumValid` is false *and* the escape-hatch button's `*ngIf` is false, so both paths are closed; and a successful offline queue writes its message into `error()`, which `app-error-state` renders with `role="alert"` in the danger colour — a safely saved acta looks like a failure. → `/impeccable clarify`, then `/impeccable harden`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (power user)**: 203 rows in the inbox, zero keyboard shortcuts, zero bulk actions, no URL-persisted filters, and 203 identical solid-blue "Revisar" buttons — the accent colour carries no information at all.

**Sam (accessibility-dependent)**: the evidence photo and the whole mobile inbox list are `div (click)` with no role, no tabindex and no key handler — keyboard-unreachable. On the acta detail, the values are invisible in the dark scheme. The state is carried by a 4px border colour with no legend. There is no `aria-live` on any outcome banner, and the app's own controls carry only the browser's default focus ring.

**Casey (distracted mobile user)**: the connection pill and the notification badge paint over the wordmark at 390px; the vote box is off-screen; step 1 has no draft autosave (only step 3 has "Guardar Borrador"); and a single phone screen shows about nine type sizes.

**Cognitive load**: 6 of the 8 checklist items fail — high load. The emotional journey peaks at login and ends at a thin "¡Acta procesada exitosamente!" with no receipt. The deepest valleys are the buried checksum, the consequence-free "Disputar" modal, the MASTER dead end and the night-shift dark scheme.

## Minor Observations

- The "En línea · Conectado" pill and the notification badge have no accessible name change with the state.
- `app.routes.ts:28` carries a hardcoded `notifications: 3` on a bell with no click handler.
- The dashboard's "ENVIADAS / Pendientes revisión" caption mislabels a sent acta as pending review.
- The progress cards expose raw database ids: "1/2 JRVs · ID 2".
- "Conexión segura…" is printed while the app is served over plain HTTP on port 80.
- `admin-usuarios` prints a raw UUID next to every email; `admin-sesiones` prints internal container addresses.
- "Mis JRVs Asignadas" should agree with the masculine "JRV", and the pending cargo uses the quietest badge variant on the screen — the state the observer must act on is the least visible.
- The two icon vocabularies (60 Ionic names in `icon-map.ts` and 11 backend keys in the sidebar) resolve to the same fallback glyph.
- `estado-badge` is exported and used by no template.
- The 35 unreferenced tokens, including the whole legacy `--sidebar-*` family, widen the surface the future dark block has to cover.
- The `p-steps` labels render run together as "VotosEvidenciaRevisión" because the app's own stepper CSS is dead.
- `transition: all` at `mis-jrvs.page.scss:48` sits on a dead rule.
